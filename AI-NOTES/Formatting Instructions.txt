Formatting Instructions

Whenever I ask for Euclidean distance calculations, K-Means, Hierarchical Clustering, or any coordinate-based calculations:

Do NOT use tables, array, aligned, cases, or any complex LaTeX environments.
Do NOT use \[...\] unless I explicitly ask.
Give everything in plain text LaTeX only.
Define the points in this exact format:
A=(170,70),\; X_A=170,\; Y_A=70

B=(172,72),\; X_B=172,\; Y_B=72
Then write the calculations step-by-step in the simplest possible form:
Distance(A,B)=\sqrt{(X_A-X_B)^2+(Y_A-Y_B)^2}

=\sqrt{(170-172)^2+(70-72)^2}

=\sqrt{(-2)^2+(-2)^2}

=\sqrt{4+4}

=\sqrt{8}

=2.83
Avoid unnecessary LaTeX commands.
Make the output easy to copy directly into my document without editing.
Keep the formatting consistent in every response.
